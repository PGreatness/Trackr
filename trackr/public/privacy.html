Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

Trackr is a browser-based job-search email organizer. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that information is handled.

Google data Trackr accesses

After you grant permission, Trackr uses read-only Gmail access to retrieve your Gmail address and messages that may relate to job applications. Trackr cannot send, edit, label, or delete email.

How data is used

Email subjects, senders, dates, and message contents are used only to identify job-search messages, classify their status, and display them to you.

Storage and retention

Gmail data and the temporary Google access token are processed in your browser and kept in the current browser tab for no longer than 24 hours. They are not transmitted to or stored on Trackr or Render servers. Disconnecting clears the session immediately. Theme preference may remain on your device.

Sharing

Trackr does not sell, share, or use Gmail data for advertising. Gmail data is sent only between your browser and Google's Gmail and identity services as needed to provide the app.

Security

Trackr requests the minimum Gmail permission needed for its features, uses HTTPS in production, and applies browser security restrictions. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can disconnect at any time in Trackr. You can also revoke Trackr from your Google Account's third-party connections page.

Google API policy

Trackr's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email displayed on Trackr's Google OAuth consent screen.
